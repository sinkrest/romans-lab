Factory Floor

Build conveyor lines to route products from inputs to outputs.

How to play

  1. Pick a level below to start
  2. Place belts on the grid — tap to place, tap same piece to rotate
  3. Connect the green inputs (left) to the matching colored outputs (right)
  4. Hit Run and watch your products flow
  5. Deliver products to the correct color bay to score points
